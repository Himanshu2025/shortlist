# Shortlist — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Shortlist is a Chrome extension that filters LinkedIn feed and search-result
posts by keywords you configure, so posts matching your skills stay visible
and others collapse.

## What Shortlist reads
Shortlist's content script reads post text, author name, and author headline
from LinkedIn pages you are already viewing, in order to apply your filter
rules to what's on screen.

## What Shortlist stores
Only your own settings — your skill list, keyword rules, and display
preferences — saved locally in your browser via the Chrome storage API.

## What Shortlist does not do
- Does not store, log, or retain any LinkedIn post, profile, or author data
- Does not transmit any data to a server — Shortlist makes no network
  requests of any kind
- Does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind
- Does not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties
